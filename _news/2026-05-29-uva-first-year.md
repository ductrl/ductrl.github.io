I have just finished my first year at the University of Virginia. A year in which too many things happened for a recap to do it justice. I got my first job, played percussion for the first time in my life, went to my first college sport games (and took part in the greatest field rush of all time), and made some lifelong friends. Although without its hardships, I definitely could not have asked for a better first year. I look forward to the upcoming years at UVA and hope that I’ll be able to do even more fun stuff and push myself further!
